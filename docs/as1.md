# Amateur Surgeon (AS1), rebuilt from its Flash original

AS1 is rebuilt on the engine from the original Flash game (Adult Swim / Mediatonic, 2008), preserved by Flashpoint in `re/flash/as1/` (`.../amateursurgeon/game.swf` and its `dialogue.xml`). It replaced the earlier port from the iPad app, archived in `port/attic/as1-ipad/` ([amateur-surgeon-1-ipad.md](amateur-surgeon-1-ipad.md)).

- **Art and sounds** are the SWF's, extracted into the package `port/games/as1/` by the build (target `as1_package`), drawn from their vectors ([engine.md](engine.md)).
- **The whole game runs on its own scripts**, the operation included: the title, intro, story scenes, level select, pre-op, the 31 patients' operations with every tool, the won and lost screens, help, credits. FFDec decompiles them into the package's `scripts/`, `port/tools/index_scripts.py` translates them (every statement), and the generic stage runs them (`src/surgeon/StageScreen.h`). There is no native module.
- **Not an emulator.** No SWF bytecode runs: the translated statements run on the engine's scene, with Flash's classes implemented by the engine.

## Why the operation runs by its scripts

Christmas's operation was first a native C++ port of its operation sprite (archived in `port/attic/christmas-native-operation/`); since 2026-10-23 it too runs by its own scripts, Christmas being the same game as AS1 in the port ([christmas.md](christmas.md)). AS1's operation (sprite 4950, the main timeline's clip `game`) is the code Christmas grew from, but of the functions both define 48 changed, and AS1 has functions and injuries of its own (the ten-rib puzzle, loose pieces, the 31 patients' special cases). Porting it as a second native module was tried first (from Christmas's, archived in `port/attic/as1-native-operation/`); running its own translated scripts instead gives every patient from the original code, and needed only general engine features, which every game now has:

- an inner clip made a node of its own when a script does to it what only a node can (`attachMovie` on the operation clip, a part of the main timeline; [engine.md](engine.md#running-the-scripts));
- `Mouse` and `Key` listeners (the operation's tools start and end on `onMouseDown`/`onMouseUp`; the number keys pick tools through `onKeyDown`);
- a plain call's `this` as AVM1's (the listener's methods calling the timeline's functions);
- `getBounds`, `createEmptyMovieClip`, buttons and text fields inside attached clips.

## Restored music

The user's own takes of AS1's sounds (`music restored/as1/` at the workspace root) are in the package in place of the movie's, put there by the build. It runs `extract_swf ... --sound <export>=<file>` for each:
- `title.mp3` replaces `theme_s` (the title, 36 s);
- `operation.mp3` replaces `operation_s` (the operation, 9.6 s);
- AS2's `female-1.mp3`–`female-4.mp3` are added as `femscream1_s`–`femscream4_s` (`--sound` with a name the movie does not export adds it), for the women's screams ([below](#corrections-to-the-originals-scripts));
- every `sfx/<linkage>.mp3` replaces the sound exported by that name (35 effects and ambiences: the tools, the screams' cues, the van, the street...). A file added there is picked up when cmake runs again (`CONFIGURE_DEPENDS`). Christmas, whose movie exports the same 35 and `operation_s`, plays these and `operation.mp3` too, but for those it has its own of ([christmas.md](christmas.md#restored-sounds)).

The scripts play them by their linkage as before. A replacement's index entry has no seek samples and no sample count (`seek 0`, `samples 0`): its LAME tag gives the decoder its encoder delay and padding, so it is played whole and loops seamlessly. Change a track by replacing its file; the build re-extracts the package.

## Corrections to the original's scripts

The scripts in `games/as1/scripts/` are the original's, decompiled; where one of them did something a player sees as a bug, it is corrected there (then `tools/index_scripts.py as1` rebuilds `scripts/index.json`), and listed here:
- **Patient 13's brain** (`DefineSprite_2299/frame_9/DoAction.as`, `DefineSprite_4950/frame_3/DoAction_8.as`, `DoAction_11.as`): the chainsaw could cut the brain through the gaps as the skull's pieces came off, and its cut line showed as soon as the skull was off. Now the skull coming off (the head's frame 9) only marks the brain `skullOff`; once the wounds the skull's removal brought out are closed too (its fragments, `surfaceExtraction` bones, and the cuts they leave; not the nerves, which the original lets be treated only once nothing covers them, the brain included, so after it), the operation's frame handler shows the brain's yellow cut line (`holder.hit._alpha = 100`) and marks it `ready`, and only then does the brain take a tool (`GetInjury`).
- **Patient 30's blood** (`DefineSprite_4950/frame_3/DoAction_26.as`): the cutter sprayed blood on the car wherever it was used, as the chainsaw's spray already did not; it no longer does there. (The car's sparks are the blood particle's own frame 9, from its frame 1 script, which the stage now runs before the particle is first drawn: [engine.md](engine.md).)
- **Patient 13's Pain Away on the brain** (`DefineSprite_4950/frame_3/DoAction_18.as`, `CheckCream`): the brain's last stage clears its `transplant` (so that its green line done removes it), and the cream then took a tenth of a point's health a frame: a stroke along the line cleared about half its points and it took several. The brain's points now go at one touch, as the head's green circle's (which keeps its `transplant`) and the lighter's on the same points.
- **Patient 15's ribs** (`frame_1/DoAction_6.as`, `missionArray15`; `DefineSprite_4950/frame_3/DoAction_5.as`): its rib stage stood on the kidneys' background (`stomach`), and the gold kidney put in two stages before stayed drawn on that body layer; it now has patient 1's and 6's rib background (`stomachrib`), and coming back to a body layer removes a finished `kidneyTransplantGold` as the original already removed a `kidneyTransplant`.
- **The women's screams** (`DefineSprite_3078`, `3082`, `3086`, `3089`: the patient's scream on a hurt): the original picked one of two sets of its male screams by patient number. Valerie (patient 13) and Aureola (26) now scream as women: one of AS2's four female screams (`femscream1_s`–`femscream4_s`, added to the package by the build from `games/as2/audio/female-<n>.mp3`), on a Sound object of `_root`'s, one scream at a time as the original's (`_root.screamPlaying`).
- **A tool let go ends, paused or not** (`DefineSprite_4950/frame_3/DoAction_3.as`, `onMouseUp`): a tool held while Esc paused the game never ended (its loop played on); now every release ends it, as Christmas's.
- **The corkscrew's yank** (`DefineSprite_4950/frame_3/DoAction_28.as`): `EndCorkscrew` played `yank` on every mouse release while the corkscrew was the tool, a click on a tool card included (Pain Away, another tool). It now plays only after a press that started using the corkscrew (`StartCorkscrew` sets `screwPressed`).

## What the engine had to do for it

Bugs a player met in AS1 that were the engine's, fixed for every game ([engine.md](engine.md), [christmas.md](christmas.md) for drawing):
- **Labels on two frames** go to the first, as Flash's goto: the extraction wound's `worm` (frame 10, the worm; again on 17, a copy of the crystal's) pulled a crystal out of patient 16, with the crystal's wound and cutting line.
- **An attached clip's frame 1** runs even when the script sends it straight on, and before it is drawn: patient 11's gold blockage left a plain cut instead of a hole (its `cutFrame` is set on frame 1), and the car's (patient 30) sparks were blood.
- **`_parent` of a clip in an unnamed holder** is the very copy it is in: the bandage's eight strips each keep their stain (they jumped about every frame).
- **A clip used as a mask is found by name**: the credits' hand wipes the ink (it stayed whole until the credits went).
- **`_alpha` set by a script replaces the timeline's opacity**, and a node flattened as a still picture keeps to its slice of depths: patient 13's brain cut line shows, above the brain.
- **Smooth motion** moves a piece going away with its own clip only: the bullet's and the magnet's holes no longer slid off up and away as they closed.
- **Per-instance playheads where copies share a path**: the pause screen lights only the button under the pointer.

## Set-up: `FlashSurgeonGame`

AS1 and Christmas are one game in the port (`src/surgeon/FlashSurgeonGame.*`): each gives a `Setup` with what is its own (AS1's: the SharedObject `user_data44885485484` as `as1_save.txt`, the pause screen's sound buttons 4827/4831 and 4811/4815, the score submission's 4978/4979 hidden, operation sprite 4950, 31 patients), and the rest is the same for both:

- The SharedObject's file and the transition variable (`fTarg`, the frame `ftb` and `bandage` go to: its art is made while they hide the screen).
- The online score submission hidden.
- The pointer clip (`pointerClip = "game.cursor"`): the operation's cursor, the tool in hand, is drawn where the mouse is at every display step, and the system pointer is hidden while it shows ([engine.md](engine.md#the-stage-a-game-run-by-its-own-scripts)). The Flash game showed the system's arrow over it.
- `_root` variables bound to the game's fields (`level`, `won`, `cause`, `lscore`...): the debug menu starts a patient by setting `level`.
- The scripts' sound manager plays the game's sounds (`SoundBank::Scripts`).

## The debug probe: `OperationProbe`

Shared with Christmas (`src/surgeon/OperationProbe.*`, given each game's operation sprite).

It reads the operation scripts' state each pass (`StageScreen::afterUpdate`) and never changes how they play.

- **The trace** (`AS_TRACE=1`), as Christmas's operation writes it, so the same play-test driver aims by it:
  - `injury created|stage|removed <make> '<frame>' stage <n> at x,y rot r glass x,y level l glassbox ... id n`, with its hit area's box (`  hitbox x0,y0,x1,y1`: a tool reaches it where the cursor's hit meets it), its hit pieces (`  hit <name> x,y`) and a puzzle's pieces and slots (`  piece n at x,y slot x,y box ...`);
  - `critter <id> at x,y` as one moves; `cut through <make> to <health> id <n>`;
  - `mission stage <n> body level <l>`; `heart stopped` / `heart started`;
  - `tool <name> down at x,y` with the patient's health, the blood the vacuum holds, where the blood lies and the cursor's hit box;
  - `follow hit x,y left n` (the corkscrew's and vacuum's moving hit); `organ <frame> on the tray at x,y for <make> at x,y`; `dropped <make> at x,y on its hit x0,y0-x1,y1: in|missed` when one carried from the tray is let go (`in` counts as progress: Christmas's five pies go in one by one, moving no stage on);
  - `mission won: score s time t`, `patient lost (<cause>)`.
- **The debug menu** (F1): any patient (one that goes on from the patient before, 24 after 23, gets the time that one began with as its `_root.storedTimeLeft`, as the scripts would have left it); in an operation, skip the stage (its injuries removed through the scripts' `RemoveInjury`), win (the last stage, then `CheckEnd`), heal, +60 s, kill, god mode (health kept at 50 or more, a minute on the clock at least).

## Testing

- **Reference:** the original in Ruffle (`port/tools/ruffle_ref.py`, `--files` the folder with `dialogue.xml`, `--so user_data44885485484 ...` for saved progress). To reach patient 1: `wait 7`, `click 180 435` (New Game), `wait 3`, `click 555 442` (skip), `wait 3`, `click 555 442`, `wait 3`, `click 530 425` (START); tool 1's card is at (128, 40).
- **Play-tests:** `port/tools/flash_playtest.py --game as1 mission<n>` plays patient n with the real tools, every stage, to "mission won" ([christmas.md](christmas.md#testing) describes the driver). What AS1's scripts need from it, as from a player:
  - the pointer moved onto a point a frame before pressing there (the live driver's every press and drag, and the scheduled scenarios' through `AS_HOVER`): the scripts' cursor clip follows the pointer on its frame (`cursor.hit` is what tools test), and a tool card's or the dialogue's `rollOver` tells the operation the pointer is over the HUD (`overBut`);
  - glass pulled where its glass and its hit area meet (`  hitbox`): the tongs grab only there;
  - the vacuum's and corkscrew's moving hit followed a frame ahead (its last move added), since the cursor clip reaches the pointer while the hit moves on;
  - critters (ants, spiders, scorpions, tapeworms...) struck two frames ahead of where they go, and never near the field's edge: a battery that misses stops the heart;
  - a transplant's piece taken from inside its hit area (in the field), a nest held under the chainsaw (twenty frames a hit, three hits), each loose piece (`  loose n x,y`) carried to the tray, a single drawn hit area sawn across;
  - the lighter worked slowly (two pixels a frame) over explosive glass: it takes ten frames of flame a piece;
  - some injuries first (`urgency`): coffee and sugar, which race the heart towards the crash (patient 18's health climbs to 200); a nest before the critters it sends (spiders that reach their mark turn to poison), shocked three times with the battery, each once its shock has played; a bomb's order (patient 25: its wires, its loose scrap, then its rods, its fuses last, anything out of turn costing time); the car's (patient 30: its bonnet off, its pipes, the blender, the starter, the bonnet back on: once the blender is in, the tongs take only from the tray); last, the newest injury first, as the scripts' `GetInjury` meets the newest first where hit areas overlap (the bomb's rods come out so, one by one);
  - every poison-like spill's drops vacuumed (`DRAINS`: poison, coffee, sugar, metal poison...), else its stage reverts;
  - a fire's (or the robot's batteries') blood taken up where the blood is (`    blood at x,y count n` at each press), from well beside its hit area (a fire grows, and over it the vacuum blows), then blown on its hit area.
- Never run the play-tests from a skip: a stage counts only when played.
- **A player's report:** with `-d`, `debug.log` (in the save folder) has the trace above and the wound log (every injury clip's place, frame and inner clips' frames, hidden ones marked, each time one changes: [engine.md](engine.md#running-the-scripts)); a wound drawn wrong or a tool's points not taking show there.

## Status

Being ported (2026-10-23). The whole game runs by its own scripts, title to end screens. Every patient's operation builds from its scripts.

- **Played to "mission won" with the real tools:** patients 1–10, 12, 14–16, 18–20, 23–27, 29 and 31 (25 of 31), rerun 2026-10-23 for 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 15 and 16; the menus' flow (`story`: a new game, the intro and story scene skipped, patient 1 played, its won scenes, the level select with the grade saved), the second patient's (`story2`: CONTINUE from a save with patient 1 done, patient 2's card, its operation, back on the level select with the marks still) and `death_final`.
- **Lost by the driver, played by hand:** 11 (the metal poison spreads faster than the driver treats it, before the blockages) and 13 (the driver carrying the old half brain to the tray lets it go on the way, so the new half is never put in; the user plays it through). Both passed the driver on 2026-10-22; that day's build now fails them the same way, so the change lies in the package or the driver, not in the engine.
- **Not yet by the driver alone:** 17, 21, 22 and 28 (spider and scorpion swarms: every critter killed or reaching its mark leaves poison, three cuts and some 25 drops each, faster than the driver clears them) and 30 (the car: every part mended in turn, but the patient bleeds out first). Their stages all progress; `god<n>` plays each with god mode on (health and clock kept up) to check every stage and the end.
- **Caution:** the build re-extracts the package (`as1_package`) when `extract_swf` changes, replacing `library.json`, `vector/` and `audio/`. With a game from the package running (a play-test), the extraction fails half-way and leaves the package broken: build only when no AS1 is running; play-tests run from copies of the exe (`build-runN`, `--build build-runN`) so the engine can be rebuilt meanwhile, but they still read the one package.
