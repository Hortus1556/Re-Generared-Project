# Amateur Surgeon 3 — the port

`AmateurSurgeon3.exe` (and its Android APK), ported from the iOS app, its final build 1.45 (`IPAs/Amateur Surgeon 3 v1.45 (iOS).ipa`; first built on 1.02, `IPAs/Amateur Surgeon 3.ipa`). The analysis is in [amateur-surgeon-3.md](amateur-surgeon-3.md). AS3 has no Flash original: the IPA is the only source. The port is in progress ([plan.md](plan.md), step 7).

### Reading the decompile

- **Names.** `re/as3/symbols.txt` names the virtual functions (from RTTI). `names_auto.txt` adds the constructors (`X::ctor`), the destructor bodies (`X::dtor`) and the factories (`X::new_<size>`, often a singleton's getter). It also files unnamed functions under a class, as `X::f_<address>`, by their neighbours in the binary or by their callers. `names.txt` holds the names worked out by hand; it wins over the rest. After editing it, run `python re/scripts/cxx_annotate.py re/as3`. Regenerate the automatic names with `cxx_ctors.py` then `cxx_units.py`.
- **Where code is.** The game's code runs from 0x3b50 to 0x30c66c; cocos2d-x starts at 0x30c66c. A call above that is identified against `re/as3/cocos2d-x-2.1.1/`.
- **Virtual calls** are labelled with the `CCNode` slot at their offset (`/*CCNode?:addChild*/`). The label is only right when the object is a node.
- **Member function pointers** (ARM's Itanium ABI) are a pair: a virtual one is (vtable offset, 1), as `schedule(0x10, 1)` is `schedule_selector(update)` (slot 4).

## The original's launch

`AppDelegate::applicationDidFinishLaunching` (`AppDelegate::vf2`, 0xc240), in order:
1. CocosDenshion's audio, and `srand(time)`.
2. The payments, social network (Game Center, its login), stats and ads singletons, then the scheduled local notifications ("come back and play") and the iCloud save sync.
3. `CCTexture2D::PVRImagesHavePremultipliedAlpha(true)`: the sheets are premultiplied.
4. The director: its EGL view, an animation interval, the 2D projection.
5. The screen's settings from the window size and content scale: 480×320 points on an iPhone, 568×320 on an iPhone 5, 512×384 on an iPad (`mt` 0x1ea7c, 0x1eb8c: 19 known screen sizes and their scale folders).
6. `runWithScene(SceneMgr)`: one root scene, which holds the game's screens (`mt::Scene`) and schedules its own `update`.
7. The `ContentManager`'s version check (online: the bundle's data is the default).

On the PC: removed (online or device-only, [rules.md](rules.md) §2) are payments, Game Center, ads, analytics (`UserTrackingMgr`, Kontagent, Bango, PlayHaven), the notifications and iCloud. The screen takes the window's shape, as the original took each device's ([below](#the-screens-shape-the-ports)), drawn from the 4.0x art.

## The screen's shape (the port's)

The original showed the middle of its 640×384 design canvas at each device's own size in canvas points: 512×384 on an iPad (4:3), 480×320 on an iPhone (3:2), 568×320 on an iPhone 5 (16:9), 533×320 and 512×320 on Android's 5:3 and 16:10 screens (its table of known screens, `ResolutionMgr.cpp`). Its layouts anchor menus and HUD to the screen's edges (TopLeft, Right, Bottom...), `setPositionOnCanvas` places the rest on the canvas. The port does the same for any window (`ResolutionMgr::fitWindow`, `sizeForAspect`), a canvas point a cocos point, 4 art pixels a point:
- **The rule** (the window's width over its height, `platform::windowAspect`): narrower than 4:3, 512×384, letterboxed above and below; from 4:3 to 16:10, 512 points wide and as high as the shape makes it (384 at 4:3, 341 at 3:2, 320 at 16:10); from 16:10 to 1.96:1, 320 high and as wide (533 at 5:3, 568 at 16:9, 600 at 15:8); wider, 626×320, letterboxed at the sides (phones at 19.5:9, 20:9, 21:9). Whole points, rounded down; the platform fills the window when the screen is within a point of its shape, so no band of a pixel or two shows.
- **The widest the art goes: 626 points.** The surgeries', dialogues', case files', pre-ops' and area maps' backgrounds are the canvas's 640 points, the title's 698, the partners' and credits' 674, the upgrades' 723 — but some menus' art stops short of the canvas's left edge, which no original device showed (the widest, 568): the upgrades' and achievements' machine is black up to canvas x 6, the world map's background starts at x 2.5 (its sheet frame trimmed from 1530 pixels, placed at −380), the title's darkened backdrop (part of its picture) at 1.75, the partners' and credits' backgrounds at about 1.8 (black there at 640 wide). So a screen shows at most canvas x 7 to 633. Found by screenshots of every screen at 640 wide (`port/tools/as3_shapes.py`, below) and the edges' pixels.
- **The phone layout on wide phones:** the surgery's two tool columns are anchored to the screen's edges, so on a 20:9 or 21:9 phone they sit at the 626-point screen's extremities, beside the bands.
- **When it changes:** at the start (`AppDelegate`) and at each `SceneMgr::replaceScene`, once the old screens are down and before the loading screen (`startWaiting`), if the window's shape asks for another size; screens pushed over (pause, popups) take their parent's. A window resized, maximised or made fullscreen, a foldable folded or unfolded, a window moved to another display: the screen up keeps its size, letterboxed, until the next screen change (screens are laid out for one size). The trace says `screen WxH canvas points (window shape a)` when it changes (and the platform `screen size WxH`, `window shape WxH (a)`).
- **What follows it:** whatever is laid out from `ResolutionMgr`'s size or the director's win size: the layouts' anchors, the popups' slides, the top bars, the surgery's HUD (the tool bar: 0.69 at 568 points, 0.68 at 512; [below](#the-surgerys-hud-on-a-big-screen-the-ports)), the creatures' bounds. The play area (`setRect(149, 45, 342, 255)`) is on the canvas, so it stays over the patient: screen y 52–307 at 320 high, 84–339 at 384. The surgery editor's bars, laid out for 568 points, and its popups wider than the screen are shrunk to fit 512 (their touches with them); its list's rows take the screen's width. The achievement banner keeps to the screen's middle.
- **As the original had them:** a carousel's next page peeks by what the screen leaves of the canvas (the save slots' New Game page half shown at 512 wide, as on an iPad; the world map's next area card); the intro movie fills the screen (aspect fill), cropping its sides at 4:3.

## Content

The original's `ContentManager` copied its content into `Documents/Downloaded/` on the first launch of each build, then asked the developer's server for newer content. The arrays of `Data/default_data.json` each went to a file (`upgrades` to `upgrades.json`, `tag_partners` to `partners.json`, `upgrade_unlocks`, `area_unlock_star` to `area_unlocks.json`, `settings`), and `Data/areas.json`, `Data/Surgeries/` and `Data/Tutorials/` were copied as they are (`copyData`, 0x272458). The game reads only those copies. 1.45 adds `free_daily_partner_list` (→ `free_daily_partner_list.json`) and drops `shop_items`. The port is offline: `readContent(name)` (`src/as3/Content.cpp`) reads a `Downloaded/` file straight from the package, as that first copy would have written it.

### Restored music

The user's own takes of some tracks (`music restored/as3/` at the workspace root) are in the package's `Sounds/` in place of the shipped ones, put there by `extract_ipa.py` (its `REPLACES` table; `--restore-music <dir>` for a package extracted before): `ingame_1.m4a`, `music_exileclinic.wav`, `music_lunacy.wav` and `music_robotopia.wav` replace the `.caf` of their name, and `super-hero-loop.m4a` replaces both `music_start` (the title, the world map, the credits) and `win_loop` (a won surgery's results), which the code now calls by the new name. `SoundMgr` finds a sound by its name: `Sounds/<name>.caf` as the original's, else the same name as `.m4a` or `.wav` (the engine decodes all three), so the surgeries' data, which name `ingame_1.caf` and the area tracks, is left as shipped.

## Animations

`mt::Animation` (0x5932c) is a node playing a `common/anim_*_anim.json`. Each clip is a Flash movie clip whose frames are the sheet frames `<clip>_<n>.png`, with a position, per-frame offsets, a depth (z = depth × 10) and control points. A clip's props are named timelines of segments (from, to, count; -1 for ever); the props of one name across the clips are one animation, as long as its longest timeline. Playing steps a cocos timer at the file's `frame_rate` (24 fps steps every 3 passes at 60 a second, as on the device). At the end of a pass it repeats, plays the next of up to 10 queued, or stops. The node's origin is the stage's bottom-left: callers place it at the registration point. Kept from the original: a backwards pass shows frames length−1..1 (the only backwards use is a creature's "out"). Guarded where the original crashed: missing frames, frames out of range, an unknown animation name (logged).

## Debug tools

- **F1 debug menu** (debug mode only, `-d`; `AmateurSurgeon3 (debug).bat` beside the exe starts it so; not in the original): `DebugMenu.h`, the engine's `DebugPanel` drawn over the cocos scene by `CCDirector::setOverlay`. While it is open, a touch begun on it is its own and Escape closes it. It opens on the surgery page when a surgery is running (even under a pause), otherwise on the go-to page.
  - **Go to:** every surgery by area, straight into play in the mode chosen (Main / Challenge 1 / Challenge 2), as `AS_SURGERY` on the save's current slot, with the slot's area, surgery and mode set as the menus would; each area's map, the title, the world map, the upgrades and partner screens (from the world map) and the credits. A running surgery is stopped first, as its quit does.
  - **Surgery** (while it is on top and not over; `SurgeryDebug.cpp`): win now (every stage counted done, then the game's own win, results and save); lose now (the heart stopped); next stage; heal (heart 100); +60 s; god mode (heart 100 and clock at least 60 s each frame, so not even a sudden-death mistake kills); tag power full; free tools (every tool button shown and on, frost cleared, also after a tutorial's pause); hit areas (each injury's points green to treat and grey done, its turned rect cyan, its pieces yellow, its place magenta). Next stage takes the stage's injuries away (finished and hidden, clamps released, tool put down, tray cleared); an injury that sends the surgery to another stage (`to_surgery_stage`, an incision) instead has its own stages completed up to its `stage_end_to_swap`, so the screen's own stage logic goes on to the next stage, the jump, or the win.
  - **Save** (labelled; writes the slot in use at once, never deletes one): +10000 coins (an earned transaction, as a win's), unlock all (every surgery 3 stars and both challenges, every tutorial completed), every upgrade, every partner, the partners' cooldowns ended; the [blood packs](#blood-packs) full, none, or the next due in 10 s.
  - **Display:** smooth motion on/off; the surgery's HUD layout; the screen's shape (Shape: window, 4:3, 16:10, 16:9, 20:9, 21:9: taken at the next screen change, as a resized window is; `platform::setAspectOverride`).

- `AS_ASPECT=<w>:<h>` (debug mode) gives the screen that shape whatever the window's (and without `AS_WINDOW` the window starts at it): `AS_ASPECT=4:3`, `16:10`, `20:9`. `AS_SCREEN=<name>[:<surgery>[:<mode>]]` (debug mode only) opens on a menu screen as the F1 menu's Go to does: `title`, `saveslots`, `world`, `upgrades`, `partners`, `achievements`, `credits`, `editor` (the list), `editor-new` (a new surgery in the editor), `area:<surgery>`, `info:<surgery>` (the case file), `dialogue:<surgery>`, `preop:<surgery>[:<mode>]` (`SurgeryDebugStart.h`), for the shape survey's screenshots.

## The menus

Title → (returning players) `SaveSlotScreen` → `WorldScreen` → `AreaScreen` → `InfoScreen` → the main surgery: `DialogueScreen` (its intro) → `PreOpScreen`; a challenge: `PreOpScreen`. The slot's `selectedSurgery` is set by the area map, `selectedMode` (1 main, 2 and 3 the challenges) by the case file. The world map's top bar is the main layout (coins, stars); the area map's is the map layout (the area's name and its stars won, of five a surgery). Ambience: the area's bgm on the ambience channel on the area map and the case file; the title, the world map and the credits play `super-hero-loop` (the user's take of the shipped `music_start`: [Restored music](#restored-music)).

- Screen changes: as in the original, every `replaceScene` goes through `LoadingScreen` (`Scene::needsLoadingScreen`, vf89, true for every screen: 0x256354, `SurgeryScreen` 0x4d234; the original showed it for one frame, then loaded, with no minimum time). The port loads the new screen behind it while the sheets decode on worker threads, the passes going on (`SceneMgr`'s `kLoading`, the port's), then uploads them a few milliseconds a pass and shows the screen. Sheets taken off are kept, so going back to a screen costs only the two passes of the change; a first visit takes 80–150 ms, going into a surgery about 220 ms (before, each change froze the old screen 0.35–0.9 s, decoding every sheet again on one thread). Under automation a change waits for the decodes in its load pass, so scripted runs repeat frame for frame (`AS_ASYNC_LOAD=1` lets them pass as for a player). A pushed screen (partner select, popups, pause) still loads at once: the first time, its first draw waits for its sheets (one 50–60 ms pass a new 4096² sheet).
- Music across the menus (the port's): a screen that fades the music out (`stopMusic(true)`: the area map, the case file, the pre-op, partner select) leaves the track playing silent, and a screen that asks for it again (the world map, the title) fades it back in where it is: the title tune is no longer restarted at each menu screen. A screen's ambience loop (`SoundMgr::playAmbience`: the area's music on its map and its case files, the partners' and achievements' tech-tree loop, the pre-op's) keeps playing if it is the one on already, where the original stopped and restarted it at every screen. A dialogue keeps what plays unless it has an ambience of its own, which takes over (the original stopped the music and the ambience at every dialogue, so one without its own was silent), and leaves it for the next screen. ## The surgery

## Surgery editor

The port's bonus: Android 1.43's cut editor (`EditorScreen` 0x446874… with `EditorMenu` and its popups), ported in `EditorScreen.cpp`, `EditorLevelsScreen.cpp`, `EditorLevel.cpp`, `EditorPlay.cpp` and `EditorWidgets.cpp`.

**Opening it.** A slot that has won Hubris 2 (`surgery_bunker_hubris2`, main mode) gets an EDITOR button on the Bunker's map. Other slots whose world map is open get SHARED: they can paste codes, import, play, copy codes and delete, but not edit.

**The list** (`EditorLevelsScreen`) shows the levels in `<save>/editedLevels/`, with Play, Edit, Code (copy) and Del for each. New surgery starts on a chest with every tool; From a patient starts from any shipped surgery. Paste code reads the clipboard; Import files reads `editedLevels/import/*.json` and `*.txt` (a code).

**The editor** (`EditorScreen`):
- The stage is drawn with its background, extra pictures and injuries, each injury built by its own class; the play area is outlined.
- Injuries and pictures are picked topmost first and dragged; injuries also turn by a handle or by ±15°/±1°, and can be copied, edited and deleted.
- A palette of the 127 injury classes of the shipped surgeries, drawn live in groups (All, Wounds, Creatures, Objects, Organs); a cell is tapped or dragged out onto the patient. Picture palettes for backgrounds (65) and extra pictures (45).
- Stages: stepping past the last adds one (the original's rule). Stage settings: background, flips, music, heart, clock, body level, next stage, delete. Injury settings: name, the stage it opens and the step that triggers it, extra injuries, "waits for" and "hidden until" dependencies; the list popup reorders them. Surgery settings: title, scream, damage multiplier, challenge, sudden death, star thresholds, tools.
- The menu: Save, Save as new version (`<id>_v<N>_<date>.json`), Test, Copy share code, Export file (`editedLevels/export/`), Versions, Discard, Delete version, Close. It works with the mouse and with touch, in the game's style (its fonts, plates).

**Playing a level.** The game's own `SurgeryScreen` plays it (the content name `edited:play`, the original's `SurgeryInfoState`+4). It has no tag partner, no tutorial, and keeps nothing: the slot is put back as it was, and pause, quit and retry note no failure. Its own results popup shows the score, the time and the stars the level's thresholds would give ("Edited surgeries give no stars or coins"), then Again, or back to the editor or the list.

**The share code.** `AS3L1:` + base64url (no padding) of zlib of the compact level JSON: the `Data/Surgeries` format without `sprite_sheets`, `sounds` and `tutorial_filename`, plus `"editor":{"format":1,"title":…}`; sprite sheets and sounds are always rebuilt from the catalog (each class's and background's first shipped surgery). Pasting ignores spaces and line breaks; another format number is refused ("made by a newer version"). Codes and files are untrusted: a 32 KB code, 128 KB of JSON and a nesting depth of 8 at most; unknown or duplicate keys, classes, pictures, music, screams and tools are refused; positions, turns, hearts, clocks, stage jumps, names and dependencies are range-checked; at most 10 stages, 50 injuries a stage, 8 pictures, 200 injuries; and a level must be playable (stage 1 with a heart, a clock, an injury and a tool). Every refusal shows a message.

## Testing

`port/tools/surgeon3_playtest.py [--build build-dev] [--keep] [--seed n] [--shots n] [--keys] [--layout top|sides] [--aspect w:h] [scenario...]` plays as a skilled player would, with the real tool buttons, taps and drags and no debug skips of the gameplay. It runs the exe hidden, fast-forwarded (`AS_FAST=1`: passes run on the pass clock as fast as they compute, about 13× faster), seeded (`AS_SEED`, printed when a run fails; `--seed` replays it exactly), on a scratch save, driven live (`AS_CONTROL`) from the debug trace only. The trace gives what a player sees:

- every stage (`stage <n>`) and every injury (`injury #<id> <class> state <state> tools <tools> at x,y rot r size w,h points x,y ... drain <d>[ waits]`: its hit points still to treat in cocos points, y up; the damage a second it does; whether it waits for others), re-traced when its stage changes or a touch wears some of it, and when it goes (`injury #<id> finished|gone`);
- the creatures' places every 5th frame (`AS_TRACE_CREATURES`), their stuns, the leeches' sizes, the worms' states;
- the heart and clock each second (`heart <h> time <t>`), the surgery and whether it is sudden death, the tool buttons (`tool <name> button x,y`) and the one selected, the pause button;
- the injuries' areas and the injection's marks, menu and cooldown; clamps and clamp points; broken halves (middle, turn, target, size); how far an embedded object sticks out and must be pulled;
- the tray and its organ (`tray organ at x,y from x,y`), the tutorial's steps and boxes, the tag partner's button and charge and its info popup's OK, bad popups, the clock's warnings, the low heart popup's OK, the end (`surgery won|lost ...`) and the lost results' buttons.

`--aspect w:h` plays at that screen shape (`AS_ASPECT`): the driver follows the trace's `screen WxH canvas points` and places the menu taps it makes by their layouts' anchors.

`port/tools/as3_shapes.py [--build dir] [--out dir] [--shapes 4:3,16:9,...] [screen...]` shoots every kind of screen (the menus by `AS_SCREEN`, a surgery with the tool bar and with the side columns, the pause, the results won and lost) at each shape (by default 4:3, 16:10, 16:9, 2:1, 20:9, 21:9) at the window's pixels, on a scratch save of a player at the whale (every area open), and puts each screen's shapes side by side in `sheet_<screen>.png`, to look for art edges, cut-off pieces and the HUD's places.
